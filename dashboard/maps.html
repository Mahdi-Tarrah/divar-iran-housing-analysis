Q8

Tehran: median apartment price per m²

The city is split into ~500 m cells (0.005°). Each cell shows the median price per m² of apartment sales, and cells with fewer than 20 ads are dropped. A plain heatmap of ads shows ad density, not price, so this map uses a price grid instead.

Reading the map

  • 881 cells with at least 20 ads each.
  • About 25–50M Toman/m² in the south and over 200M in the north.
  • Median price per cell vs latitude: r = 0.78, "the further north, the more expensive".
  • Hover a cell: price M/m², n = ads.
Q9

Amenities across Iran

One layer per amenity. Each city sits at the median coordinate of its own ads, and only cities with at least 200 answers are shown. Colours are relative to the top city for that amenity. Hover for the real percentage.

Reading the map

  • Use the layer control (top right) to switch between balcony, elevator, guard, barbecue and pool.
  • Balcony / elevator are strongest in the new towns around Tehran (Pardis, Parand, Andisheh).
  • Guard / barbecue / pool are strongest along the Caspian coast (Chamestan, Nur, Ramsar, Salman Shahr).
  • The Iran border has a 5 km buffer so that coastal cities such as Bandar Abbas and Konarak are not dropped.
