What one million Divar listings say about Iran's housing market

I took 1,000,000 real-estate ads from Divar (61 columns: location, size, year built, amenities, sale price, deposit and rent) and asked three questions: what does the market actually look like, can similar listings be grouped into a recommender, and can a fair rent and sale price be predicted?

The dataset at a glance

Data coverage

  • 65.6% of ads have coordinates (655,608).
  • 56.8% have a sale price, and 81.6% a build year.
  • Prices range up to 1014 Toman and sizes up to 107 m², which is why cleaning is done on the log scale.
  • Time span: Khordad 1401 to Bahman 1403. Most ads fall between Ordibehesht and Azar 1403.

Project goal

The project has three parts. Each one builds on the cleaning rules worked out in the one before.

📊

1 · Statistical analysis

Nine descriptive questions (categories, build year, seasonality, prices, geography, correlation, amenities) and four hypothesis tests about common beliefs on the housing market.
🧭

2 · Recommender system

Cluster listings with K-means and DBSCAN, then recommend listings from the same cluster: either ones similar to an ad you clicked or ones that match your budget and preferred location.
🎯

3 · Price prediction

Two separate XGBoost regressions: one predicts monthly rent and one predicts sale price, with a strict train / validation / test protocol and no data leakage.

How we worked

The same pipeline runs under every part of the project.

1

Load & type

Read only the needed columns. Convert Persian digits, Jalali months and mixed true/"true"/"unselect" flags.

2

Clean

IQR fence on the log of price and size. It removes typos like 1014 Toman or 10,000,000 m² but keeps real luxury homes.

3

Explore

Descriptive charts, maps and Pearson/Spearman correlations, broken down by city and property type.

4

Test

Welch t-test + Mann-Whitney, Cohen's d, per-type re-tests (Simpson's paradox), Bonferroni correction.

5

Model

K-means / DBSCAN on price + UTM, then XGBoost on log price. Group statistics are computed from the train split only.

Headline findings

The results most worth remembering. Each page has the details and interactive charts.

📊 Market structure

  • 83% of ads are residential, and residential sale ads are about twice residential rent ads. apartment-sell alone is 30% of the data.
  • Rent ads are seasonal: they peak in Tir and Mordad at over 52k per month. Sale ads stay roughly flat.
  • Build year shows heaping on round years (1380, 1385, 1390…), so users type approximate years.
  • Tehran alone has 190,904 ads, followed by Mashhad (69k) and Karaj (49k).

🔗 Relationships

  • Across the whole country, price and size correlate at only 0.22. Within Tehran apartments it rises to 0.81, because mixing cities and property types was hiding the relationship.
  • "The further north, the more expensive": the median price per m² in a Tehran grid cell correlates 0.78 with latitude.
  • Balcony and elevator are near 100% in new towns. Guard, barbecue and pool cluster on the northern coast.

🧪 Beliefs we tested

  • "Homes in megacities are smaller": statistically significant but Cohen's d = 0.016. Compared within each property type the result reverses, which is Simpson's paradox.
  • "Old homes were roomier": not supported — new homes are 17 m² larger (p = 1.0, d = 0.35).
  • Listings with all four luxury amenities sell for about 2× the average (11.2B vs 5.6B Toman, p ≈ 0.0001).

🤖 Models

  • "Price + UTM coordinates" was the best feature set for clustering. Every extra feature made the clusters worse.
  • The final k = 7 was chosen with KneeLocator, three quality metrics and a profile comparison. DBSCAN finds 3 market poles: Tehran-Karaj-Isfahan, Mashhad and Shiraz.
  • Rent model: Test R² 0.602. Sale model: Test R² 0.606, MAE ≈ 1.36B Toman.

How it was built

Every figure on this dashboard is produced by a notebook in the repository and can be reproduced end to end.

Data & statistics

pandas · NumPy · SciPy — cleaning on the log scale, Welch's t-test, Mann-Whitney U, Cohen's d, Bonferroni correction

Machine learning

scikit-learn · XGBoost — K-means, DBSCAN, gradient boosting with target encoding

Geospatial

GeoPandas · Folium · utm — UTM zone 39 projection, grid aggregation, interactive maps

This dashboard

Static HTML, CSS and Chart.js — no build step and no server, opens straight from the file system

Notes on the data. All amounts are in Toman. Months are converted to the Jalali calendar from the first day of each Gregorian month.
